The Goal Cascading Tool

This tool connects organizational priorities across three levels: Company Strategic Goals, Department Goals, and Business Function Goals — so every team can see how their work ladders up to company strategy.

Company Strategic Goals
Define the organization's highest-level priorities and desired business outcomes.

Department Goals
Define how each department will contribute to achieving company strategic goals.
Align
Business Function Goals
Define the specific outcomes each business function will deliver to support its department goals.

The tool helps teams translate company strategy into clear, measurable goals throughout the organization. Departments align their goals to the company strategic goals they support, and business functions create goals that contribute to their department's priorities.

This structure creates a clear line of sight from company strategy → department priorities → business function execution, helping leaders understand how work across the organization contributes to broader business outcomes.
