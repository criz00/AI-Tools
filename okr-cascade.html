Strategic Alignment

The OKR Cascading Tool

This tool connects OKRs across the company, department, and business function levels, so teams can see how their work supports company goals.

This tool turns company strategy into clear, measurable OKRs across the organization. Departments connect their objectives to company key results, and business functions connect their objectives to department key results.

Each key result includes a start, target, and current value, making it easy to track progress and see how work at each level supports company goals.

Note: OKR structures and alignment will vary by organization. This prototype shows one example of how it can work. If you'd like to see how this could work for your organization, let's connect.
