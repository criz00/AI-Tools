Learning & Performance Prioritization Framework
Reviewer mode is on. Every screen is unlocked, and Next always appears so you can move freely.

Key Terms

Priority Score
The number the framework calculates from your answers. A higher score means the request needs attention sooner.
Priority Label
The category that goes with a score: Critical (8+), High (6–7), Medium (3–5) or Low (2 or less).
Factor
One thing the framework measures, such as Frequency or Deadline. Each factor is worth 0 to 3 points.
Proficiency
The point at which someone can perform a task correctly and consistently without help.
Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR)
The predictable revenue the company earns each month from sources like subscriptions, service contracts or ongoing client accounts.
Needs Analysis
The early conversation with a stakeholder where you learn what's driving a request, who it affects and what's at stake.
Job Aid
A checklist, quick-reference guide or similar tool people use while doing a task, instead of relying on memory.
